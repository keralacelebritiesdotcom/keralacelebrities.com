The story

Who is Dulquer Salmaan?

Dulquer Salmaan is an Indian actor, playback singer and film producer who has built a distinctive career across Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu and Hindi cinema. The son of veteran Malayalam actor Mammootty, he entered films with Second Show in 2012 and quickly established an identity of his own.

Before entering cinema, Dulquer studied business management at Purdue University and worked as a business manager in Dubai. He also trained at the Barry John Acting Studio before beginning his film career.

Early successes such as Ustad Hotel, ABCD, Neelakasham Pachakadal Chuvanna Bhoomi and Bangalore Days helped establish him as a leading young actor in Malayalam cinema. His performance in Charlie earned the Kerala State Film Award for Best Actor.

He later expanded his presence through Tamil films such as O Kadhal Kanmani, Telugu films including Mahanati, Sita Ramam and Lucky Baskhar, and the Hindi films Karwaan and Chup: Revenge of the Artist. He also founded Wayfarer Films, adding film production to his creative career.

At a glance

  • Born on 28 July 1983 in Kochi, Kerala.
  • Made his acting debut with Second Show in 2012.
  • Received the Filmfare Award for Best Male Debut – South for Ustad Hotel.
  • Won the Kerala State Film Award for Best Actor for Charlie.
  • Received Filmfare Critics recognition for Kali and Kammatipaadam.
  • Won Filmfare Critics Awards for his Telugu performances in Mahanati and Sita Ramam.
  • Works across Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu and Hindi cinema.
  • Founded Wayfarer Films as a production company in 2019.
Career milestones

A career built across languages

2012

Second Show

Dulquer made his screen debut with the Malayalam film Second Show, beginning a career that would soon move across genres and languages.

2012

Ustad Hotel

His performance in Ustad Hotel brought wider recognition and earned him the Filmfare Award for Best Male Debut – South.

2013–14

ABCD to Bangalore Days

Films including ABCD, Neelakasham Pachakadal Chuvanna Bhoomi, Vaayai Moodi Pesavum, Bangalore Days and Vikramadithyan expanded his audience and established his youthful leading-man image.

2015

O Kadhal Kanmani & Charlie

Dulquer strengthened his Tamil presence with Mani Ratnam's O Kadhal Kanmani and delivered the acclaimed performance in Charlie that later earned him Kerala State Best Actor honours.

2016–17

Kali & Kammatipaadam

His performances in Kali and Kammatipaadam showed a more intense and physically demanding side, earning him Filmfare Critics recognition.

2018

Mahanati

His portrayal of Gemini Ganesan in the Telugu biographical drama Mahanati broadened his profile nationally and brought Filmfare Critics recognition for Best Actor – Telugu.

2019–20

Production & wider Indian cinema

With Wayfarer Films, Dulquer expanded into production while continuing to work in Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu and Hindi projects.

2021–22

Kurup & Sita Ramam

Kurup became a major Malayalam release, followed by the Telugu romantic drama Sita Ramam, which became a major commercial and cultural success and earned him another Filmfare Critics Award.

2024

Lucky Baskhar

The Telugu period crime drama Lucky Baskhar added another widely recognised performance to his multilingual filmography and continued his run as a pan-Indian leading actor.

2025–26

Continuing across industries

Dulquer continues to develop projects across Indian languages, balancing acting, production and his long-standing interest in cinema beyond a single regional industry.

Beyond acting

Singer, producer and entrepreneur

Dulquer's career extends beyond acting. He has performed as a playback singer in Malayalam and other South Indian films, adding music to a creative profile already built around performance.

In 2019, he established Wayfarer Films, a production company associated with films including Sufiyum Sujatayum, Kurup, Salute and other contemporary projects. His production work has helped create another route into Malayalam cinema alongside his acting career.

He is also known for his interest in automobiles and for maintaining a strong connection with audiences across multiple Indian-language film industries.
