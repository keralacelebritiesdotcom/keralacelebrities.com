The story

Who is Nivin Pauly?

Nivin Pauly is an Indian actor and producer who has become an important figure in contemporary Malayalam cinema. His performances are often associated with an approachable, understated screen presence, while his film choices have taken him across romance, comedy, family drama, action and more experimental material.

After studying engineering and working professionally before entering cinema, he made his acting debut in Vineeth Sreenivasan's Malarvaadi Arts Club (2010). A run of successful films soon established him as one of the most recognisable actors of his generation.

Thattathin Marayathu, Neram, Ohm Shanthi Oshaana, Bangalore Days and especially Premam helped define his popularity during the 2010s. He subsequently moved into more varied roles, including the critically acclaimed Moothon, while continuing to work in mainstream Malayalam cinema and selected Tamil projects.

At a glance

  • Born on 11 October 1984 in Aluva, Kerala.
  • Screen debut in Malarvaadi Arts Club (2010).
  • Breakthrough with Thattathin Marayathu (2012).
  • Premam (2015) became a defining film of his career.
  • Won the Kerala State Film Award for Best Actor for Moothon.
  • Founded the production company Pauly Jr. Pictures.
  • Continues to work across Malayalam and other Indian-language cinema.
Career milestones

A career shaped by memorable characters

2010

Malarvaadi Arts Club

Nivin made his screen debut in Vineeth Sreenivasan's ensemble comedy Malarvaadi Arts Club.

2012

Thattathin Marayathu

The romantic drama became a major turning point and established Nivin as one of Malayalam cinema's leading young stars.

2013–14

Neram, 1983 and Bangalore Days

Films including Neram, 1983 and Bangalore Days demonstrated his ability to move between comedy, romance and grounded character-driven roles.

2015

Premam

Premam became a cultural phenomenon and one of the most influential Malayalam films of its era, further cementing Nivin's popularity.

2016

Action Hero Biju

With Action Hero Biju, Nivin explored a police-procedural format while also taking on production responsibilities.

2019

Moothon

His performance in Geetu Mohandas's Moothon marked a significant shift into darker and more demanding material and earned the Kerala State Film Award for Best Actor.

2022–24

Wider range of roles

Nivin continued to experiment with genres and scale, including Padavettu, Thuramukham and other projects while maintaining a strong Malayalam audience base.

2026

Bethlehem Kudumba Unit

Nivin returned to a major commercial success with Bethlehem Kudumba Unit, which became a major Onam-season hit and a significant box-office milestone in his career.

Beyond acting

Producer and creative collaborator

Nivin has also worked as a producer through Pauly Jr. Pictures, backing projects alongside his acting career. His production work reflects an interest in supporting films and filmmakers beyond conventional star vehicles.

His career has also included work in Tamil cinema, allowing him to build a presence beyond Malayalam while remaining closely associated with the contemporary Malayalam film movement.
