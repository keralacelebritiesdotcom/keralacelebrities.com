Prithviraj Sukumaran

Prithviraj Sukumaran is an actor, director, producer and playback singer whose career has grown from a young leading man into one of Malayalam cinema's prominent multi-hyphenate film personalities. Known for moving between commercial entertainers, performance-driven dramas and ambitious filmmaking, he has built a career across Malayalam and other Indian-language industries.

Who is Prithviraj Sukumaran?

Prithviraj Sukumaran was born on 16 October 1982 in Thiruvananthapuram to actors Sukumaran and Mallika Sukumaran. He grew up around cinema and completed his schooling in Kerala after spending part of his early education in Tamil Nadu. He later travelled to Australia for higher studies, but his entry into Malayalam cinema changed the direction of his career.

Director Ranjith cast him in Nandanam, which became his acting debut in 2002. His early filmography included a mixture of romantic dramas, action films and character-oriented projects. Classmates became a major turning point in 2006, while his performance in Vaasthavam earned him the Kerala State Film Award for Best Actor.

Over time, Prithviraj expanded his work beyond acting. He became a producer, established Prithviraj Productions with Supriya Menon, and moved behind the camera as the director of Lucifer. His career now spans acting, filmmaking, production, distribution and playback singing.

Career Milestones

1982

Born in Thiruvananthapuram

Prithviraj Sukumaran was born on 16 October 1982 to actors Sukumaran and Mallika Sukumaran.

2002

Nandanam

Prithviraj made his Malayalam acting debut with Ranjith's Nandanam, beginning a career that would soon develop across several Indian film industries.

2005–06

Breakthrough and wider recognition

His Tamil work began with Kana Kandein, followed by films such as Parijatham and Mozhi. In Malayalam, Classmates became a major commercial success and Vaasthavam brought his first Kerala State Film Award for Best Actor.

2009

Playback singing

Prithviraj added playback singing to his film work, including the song “Puthiya Mukham” from the film of the same name.

2010–11

Producer and wider Indian cinema

He entered film production through August Cinema and co-produced films including Urumi and Indian Rupee. Indian Rupee won the National Film Award for Best Feature Film in Malayalam.

2012–13

Major acting recognition

His performances in Ayalum Njanum Thammil and Celluloid earned him another Kerala State Film Award for Best Actor, strengthening his reputation for balancing commercial and serious cinema.

2017–19

Prithviraj Productions and Lucifer

Prithviraj and Supriya Menon launched Prithviraj Productions. In 2019, Prithviraj made his directorial debut with Lucifer, starring Mohanlal, while continuing to act and produce.

2023–24

Salaar and Aadujeevitham

His pan-Indian profile grew through Salaar, while Aadujeevitham – The Goat Life became a major performance milestone. His portrayal of Najeeb earned him the Kerala State Film Award for Best Actor at the 54th Kerala State Film Awards.

2025

Empuraan

Prithviraj returned as director for Empuraan, the second film in the Lucifer franchise. The Central Board of Film Certification lists him as the director of the 2025 film.

Director, Producer and Playback Singer

Prithviraj has deliberately built a career that extends beyond acting. Prithviraj Productions, founded with Supriya Menon, was created with the aim of facilitating films that contribute to Malayalam cinema. The company made its production debut with 9 in 2019 and has since been involved in production and distribution.

As a director, Prithviraj's first feature was Lucifer (2019). He followed it with Empuraan (2025), again working with Mohanlal and an ensemble cast. His work behind the camera has become an important part of his identity within contemporary Malayalam cinema.

Why Prithviraj matters

Prithviraj represents a generation of Malayalam filmmakers who move fluidly between acting, production and direction. His career combines mainstream visibility with projects that demand physical transformation, complex character work and ambitious filmmaking.

Prithviraj Sukumaran FAQ

Who is Prithviraj Sukumaran?

Prithviraj Sukumaran is an Indian actor, director, producer and playback singer, best known for his work in Malayalam cinema.

When was Prithviraj Sukumaran born?

He was born on 16 October 1982 in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala.

What was Prithviraj's acting debut?

His Malayalam acting debut was Nandanam, released in 2002.

How many Kerala State Film Awards for Best Actor has he won?

He has won the Best Actor award three times, for Vaasthavam, Ayalum Njanum Thammil and Celluloid, and Aadujeevitham – The Goat Life. The latter brought his third Best Actor win.

What was his directorial debut?

Lucifer (2019), starring Mohanlal, marked Prithviraj's feature-film directorial debut.
