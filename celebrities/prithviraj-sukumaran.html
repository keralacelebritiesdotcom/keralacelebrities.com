Prithviraj Sukumaran

Prithviraj Sukumaran is an actor, director, producer and playback singer whose career has grown from a young leading man into one of Malayalam cinema's prominent multi-hyphenate film personalities. His work spans mainstream entertainers, performance-driven dramas and ambitious filmmaking across Indian-language cinema.

The story

Who is Prithviraj Sukumaran?

Prithviraj Sukumaran was born on 16 October 1982 in Thiruvananthapuram to actors Sukumaran and Mallika Sukumaran. He grew up around Malayalam cinema and later travelled to Australia for higher studies before an opportunity in film changed the direction of his life.

Director Ranjith cast him in Nandanam, which became his Malayalam acting debut in 2002. His early filmography moved across romantic dramas, action films and character-oriented projects. Classmates became a major turning point, while Vaasthavam brought his first Kerala State Film Award for Best Actor.

Over time, Prithviraj expanded beyond acting. He became a producer, co-founded Prithviraj Productions with Supriya Menon and moved behind the camera as director of Lucifer. His career now encompasses acting, directing, production, distribution and playback singing.

At a glance

  • Born on 16 October 1982 in Thiruvananthapuram.
  • Son of actor Sukumaran and actress Mallika Sukumaran.
  • Malayalam acting debut with Nandanam in 2002.
  • Won the Kerala State Film Award for Best Actor for Vaasthavam.
  • Won Best Actor again for Ayalum Njanum Thammil and Celluloid.
  • Won his third Best Actor state award for Aadujeevitham.
  • Made his feature directorial debut with Lucifer in 2019.
  • Co-founded Prithviraj Productions.
Career milestones

A career shaped by ambition and reinvention

1982

Born in Thiruvananthapuram

Prithviraj Sukumaran was born on 16 October 1982 to actors Sukumaran and Mallika Sukumaran.

2002

Nandanam

Prithviraj made his Malayalam acting debut with Ranjith's Nandanam, beginning a career that would soon develop across several Indian film industries.

2005–06

Tamil work and breakthrough

He made his Tamil debut with Kana Kandein and followed it with films including Parijatham and Mozhi. In Malayalam, Classmates became a major success and Vaasthavam earned him his first Kerala State Film Award for Best Actor.

2009

Playback singing

Prithviraj added playback singing to his film work, including the title song from Puthiya Mukham.

2011

Indian Rupee and production

He acted in and co-produced Ranjith's Indian Rupee, which won the National Film Award for Best Feature Film in Malayalam.

2012–13

Two major acting performances

His work in Ayalum Njanum Thammil and Celluloid earned him the Kerala State Film Award for Best Actor for the award year.

2017–19

Prithviraj Productions and Lucifer

Prithviraj and Supriya Menon established Prithviraj Productions. In 2019, he made his feature-film directorial debut with Lucifer, starring Mohanlal.

2023–24

Salaar and Aadujeevitham

His pan-Indian profile expanded through Salaar, while Aadujeevitham – The Goat Life became a major performance milestone. The role of Najeeb earned him his third Kerala State Film Award for Best Actor.

2025

Empuraan

Prithviraj returned as director for Empuraan, the second film in the Lucifer franchise, while continuing to act and produce.

Beyond acting

Director, producer and cultural presence

Prithviraj has deliberately built a career that extends beyond acting. Prithviraj Productions, founded with Supriya Menon, has become an important part of his work in contemporary Malayalam cinema.

As a director, his first feature was Lucifer in 2019. He followed it with Empuraan in 2025, continuing the franchise while working with Mohanlal and a large ensemble cast.

His ability to move between acting, production and direction has made him one of the more visible multi-hyphenate figures in contemporary Malayalam cinema.

Why Prithviraj matters

  • He has combined leading-actor stardom with demanding character work.
  • His career spans Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu and Hindi-language cinema.
  • He has won the Kerala State Film Award for Best Actor three times.
  • His directorial work began with Lucifer and continued with Empuraan.
  • His production work has expanded his influence beyond performance alone.
