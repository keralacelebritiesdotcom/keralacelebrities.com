The story

Who is Mohanlal?

Mohanlal is an Indian actor, producer, playback singer and director whose work has been central to the evolution of modern Malayalam cinema. His strength as a performer lies in his ability to make very different characters feel lived-in rather than merely performed.

He entered the film world at a young age and made his widely recognised screen debut in Manjil Virinja Pookkal (1980), initially appearing as an antagonist. From there, his career expanded through supporting roles and leading performances, eventually making him one of the most influential stars in Indian cinema.

What makes his filmography particularly distinctive is its range. He has moved from comedy and family drama to tragedy, crime, action, psychological stories and experimental cinema, while repeatedly returning to characters rooted in ordinary Malayalam life.

His body of work includes films that became cultural reference points in Kerala, as well as performances that brought Malayalam cinema wider national and international attention.

At a glance

  • Born on 21 May 1960.
  • Screen debut in Manjil Virinja Pookkal (1980).
  • Five National Film Awards, according to the Government of India.
  • Padma Shri recipient in 2001.
  • Padma Bhushan recipient in 2019.
  • Honorary Lieutenant Colonel in the Territorial Army from 2009.
  • Dadasaheb Phalke Award recipient for the year 2023, conferred in 2025.
  • Directed Barroz, his first feature as director.
Career milestones

A career measured in turning points

1978

Early beginning

Mohanlal acted in Thiranottam, a film whose release was delayed for many years. His recognised screen career would begin with a different film soon after.

1980

Manjil Virinja Pookkal

His widely recognised screen debut came in the romance film Manjil Virinja Pookkal, where he played the antagonist.

1986

Rise to major stardom

Rajavinte Makan became a major turning point and helped establish Mohanlal as a leading star. His work with filmmakers such as Sathyan Anthikad also strengthened his man-next-door image.

1989

Kireedam and national recognition

His performance as Sethumadhavan in Kireedam earned a Special Jury Mention at the National Film Awards and remains one of the most discussed performances of his career.

1991

Bharatham

His performance in Bharatham earned the National Film Award for Best Actor, reinforcing his reputation for handling emotionally demanding roles.

1997–2000

From Iruvar to Vanaprastham

Iruvar, Guru and especially Vanaprastham demonstrated his ability to move beyond conventional commercial stardom. Vanaprastham brought National Film Award recognition for both acting and production.

2009

Territorial Army honour

Mohanlal was granted the honorary rank of Lieutenant Colonel in the Indian Territorial Army, a distinction that added a unique public dimension to his career.

2016

Pulimurugan

Pulimurugan became a major commercial milestone in Malayalam cinema and further expanded the scale of Mohanlal's mainstream appeal.

2019

Padma Bhushan

The Government of India honoured Mohanlal with the Padma Bhushan for his contribution to the arts.

2025

Dadasaheb Phalke Award

The Government of India announced Mohanlal as the recipient of the Dadasaheb Phalke Award for 2023. The President of India conferred the honour at the 71st National Film Awards ceremony on 23 September 2025.

Beyond acting

Producer, singer and director

Mohanlal's contribution to cinema extends beyond acting. He has worked as a film producer and playback singer, and his production work includes major Malayalam films.

His directorial journey reached a new stage with Barroz, his first feature film as director. The move added another dimension to a career already spanning acting, production and music.

His work in multiple Indian languages has also helped carry Malayalam cinema and its performers into a broader national conversation.

Why Mohanlal matters

  • He built a rare bridge between commercial stardom and character-driven cinema.
  • His performances range from understated humour to intense tragedy and action.
  • Several of his characters have become part of Kerala's popular cultural memory.
  • His national honours reflect a career that has extended well beyond Malayalam cinema's traditional boundaries.
Frequently asked questions

Mohanlal FAQ

Who is Mohanlal?

Mohanlal is an Indian actor, producer, playback singer and director, best known for his work in Malayalam cinema and his performances in several other Indian-language films.

When was Mohanlal born?

Mohanlal was born on 21 May 1960.

What is Mohanlal's biggest national honour?

Mohanlal received the Dadasaheb Phalke Award for the year 2023. The President of India conferred it at the 71st National Film Awards ceremony on 23 September 2025.

How many National Film Awards has Mohanlal won?

The Government of India describes Mohanlal as a recipient of five National Film Awards, covering acting and film production recognition.

What are some of Mohanlal's most famous films?

Frequently cited landmark films include Kireedam, Bharatham, Devasuram, Spadikam, Manichitrathazhu, Iruvar, Vanaprastham, Thanmatra, Drishyam, Pulimurugan and Lucifer.

What is Mohanlal's nickname?

He is widely known to audiences in Kerala as “Lalettan”, while “The Complete Actor” is a prominent description associated with his career.
