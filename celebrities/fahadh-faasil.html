The story

Who is Fahadh Faasil?

Fahadh Faasil is an Indian actor and film producer whose work has become closely associated with the modern wave of Malayalam cinema. He is the son of filmmaker Fazil and began his acting career with his father's Kaiyethum Doorath in 2002.

After his early debut, Fahadh stepped away from cinema for several years while pursuing his education in the United States. He returned to the screen with Kerala Cafe in 2009, beginning a second phase of his career that would establish him as one of Malayalam cinema's most distinctive performers.

Films such as Chaappa Kurishu, Akam, 22 Female Kottayam, Diamond Necklace, Amen, North 24 Kaatham and Artist demonstrated his willingness to move between radically different characters.

His later work expanded his reach through films including Maheshinte Prathikaaram, Thondimuthalum Driksakshiyum, Kumbalangi Nights, Joji, Malik, Vikram and Aavesham. Alongside acting, he has developed a parallel identity as a producer.

At a glance

  • Born on 8 August 1982 in Alappuzha, Kerala.
  • Made his screen debut in Kaiyethum Doorath (2002).
  • Returned to cinema with Kerala Cafe (2009).
  • Won the Kerala State Film Award for Second Best Actor for Chaappa Kurishu and Akam.
  • Won the Kerala State Film Award for Best Actor for Artist and North 24 Kaatham.
  • Won the National Film Award for Best Supporting Actor for Thondimuthalum Driksakshiyum.
  • Won the Kerala State Film Award for Best Character Actor for Kumbalangi Nights.
  • Works as both actor and producer.
Career milestones

A career built on reinvention

2002

Kaiyethum Doorath

Fahadh made his screen debut in his father Fazil's romantic film Kaiyethum Doorath. After the film, he took a long break from acting while continuing his studies.

2009

Kerala Cafe

He returned to cinema through the anthology Kerala Cafe, marking the beginning of the second phase of his acting career.

2011

Chaappa Kurishu & Akam

His performances in Chaappa Kurishu and Akam earned him the Kerala State Film Award for Second Best Actor and signalled his emergence as a serious performer.

2012–13

22 Female Kottayam to Artist

22 Female Kottayam, Diamond Necklace, Annayum Rasoolum, Amen, North 24 Kaatham and Artist established his range and brought major critical and industry recognition.

2013

Kerala State Best Actor

Fahadh won the Kerala State Film Award for Best Actor for his performances in Artist and North 24 Kaatham.

2014–17

From Iyobinte Pusthakam to Thondimuthalum Driksakshiyum

His work in Iyobinte Pusthakam, Maheshinte Prathikaaram and Thondimuthalum Driksakshiyum further strengthened his reputation for character-driven performances.

2018

National Film Award

Fahadh received the National Film Award for Best Supporting Actor for his performance in Thondimuthalum Driksakshiyum.

2019–20

Kumbalangi Nights

As both performer and producer, Kumbalangi Nights became an important part of his growing production profile. He received the Kerala State Film Award for Best Character Actor for the film.

2021–24

Joji, Malik, Vikram and Aavesham

His later work continued to move across languages and genres, including Joji, Malik, the Tamil film Vikram and the Malayalam blockbuster Aavesham.

2026

Continuing across industries

Fahadh continues to work across Malayalam, Tamil and Telugu cinema, with new acting and production projects adding to an already diverse filmography.

Beyond acting

Producer and creative collaborator

Fahadh's contribution to Malayalam cinema extends into production. Through his producing work, he has backed films that reflect the same preference for distinctive stories and strong character writing that marks his acting choices.

His production credits include films such as Iyobinte Pusthakam, Maheshinte Prathikaaram, Take Off, Kumbalangi Nights, C U Soon, Joji, Thankam, Premalu and other projects.

His work in Tamil and Telugu cinema has also broadened his profile beyond Malayalam, while his Malayalam film choices remain central to his identity as a performer.

Why Fahadh Faasil matters

  • He is known for moving between lead, supporting and unconventional characters without relying on one fixed screen persona.
  • His post-2009 career helped define a new generation of Malayalam character-driven stardom.
  • His National Film Award recognised his supporting performance in Thondimuthalum Driksakshiyum.
  • His work as a producer has connected him with several acclaimed contemporary Malayalam films.
  • His performances in other Indian languages have expanded his reach while retaining a strong Malayalam-cinema base.
