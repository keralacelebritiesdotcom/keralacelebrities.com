Our Story • Our Legacy • Our Future

More Than a Website.
A Kerala Legacy.

KeralaCelebrities.com was registered on 24 December 2008. What began with a simple idea—to create a dedicated online space for Kerala's celebrated personalities—has a history that reaches back to the early days of social entertainment on the web.

2008Our journey began
24December 2008
A date worth remembering

Built with a long-term vision for Kerala.

The KeralaCelebrities.com domain was registered on 24 December 2008. That date is more than a registration milestone—it is the beginning of the identity and legacy behind this project.

Long before today's creator economy and short-form entertainment culture, the vision was to build a recognizable home for information and stories connected with Kerala's celebrities and entertainment world.

Our legacy starts on 24 December 2008. We carry that history forward while building a modern destination for a new generation of Kerala audiences.
The journey

From a domain name to a destination

KeralaCelebrities.com is being shaped around three simple ideas: preserve the past, celebrate the present and build something valuable for the future.

01 / THE BEGINNING

24 December 2008

The KeralaCelebrities.com domain was registered, establishing the name and the foundation for a Kerala-focused entertainment identity.

02 / THE EVOLUTION

A changing entertainment world

Kerala's cinema, television, music and celebrity culture continued to evolve. The opportunity to document and celebrate that world only became greater.

03 / THE NEXT CHAPTER

Digital, social & video

Today, the vision extends across the website and YouTube, bringing celebrity stories, profiles, features and entertainment content to a wider audience.
